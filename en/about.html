About People Stationery

Why I started a stationery YouTube channel

A friend inspired me to start creating stationery videos on YouTube.

My friend does not make stationery videos, but is active on YouTube.

I watched my friend struggle while pursuing that activity, and thought, “I want to become a YouTuber too, so I can better understand what my friend is going through.” That was one of the reasons I decided to start.

Why build this website?

To put it simply,

I thought, “We’ve reached 50 subscribers, so why not build a website and share Studio People with more people?”

That was the idea behind it.

I started building this website on September 1, 2026. It was the day I came home after the school term-opening ceremony.

It was also the day after the channel passed 50 subscribers.

I had started uploading videos near the beginning of the first school term, so building a website felt like a good milestone.
