How we handle information

Effective date: October 22, 2026

Studio People (“the Site”) is committed to handling information responsibly. This policy explains the information that may be processed when you use the Site.

1. Information We May Collect

  • Email address needed to subscribe to article-update notifications.
  • Information needed to confirm subscriptions, manage delivery status, and process unsubscribes.
  • If a contact form is published in the future, information entered in that form, such as name, email address, and message.
  • Access information that hosting or service providers may process when delivering and operating the Site, which may include IP address, access time, and browser information.

This policy does not mean that information fields or features that are not currently available are already being collected or operated.

2. Purposes of Use

Information may be used to send article-update notifications and subscription confirmations, manage unsubscribes and prevent further emails, respond to inquiries if an inquiry feature becomes available, maintain and secure the Site, address misuse, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. Email Notifications and Unsubscribing

If you subscribe to article-update notifications, we may send emails to confirm your subscription and notify you of article updates. Where an unsubscribe method is provided in an email or on the Site, follow the instructions to stop receiving notifications.

After unsubscribing, limited status information may be retained for a period to prevent accidental re-subscription or further delivery. The retention period and deletion process will be reviewed to reflect actual operations. This statement does not mean that automatic deletion is already implemented.

4. Third-Party Services

The Site may use GitHub Pages, Supabase, and Google services (including Google Fonts, Google Apps Script, and Gmail) to host the Site, manage subscription information, send email, and display fonts. These providers may process information as necessary to provide their services, under their own policies and terms.

  • GitHub Privacy Statement
  • Supabase Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy

5. Analytics and Cookies

The Site does not currently use a dedicated analytics tool such as Google Analytics. However, external services used to publish the Site may process access information for security and service delivery. Cookies or similar technologies may also be used depending on the behavior of those services.

6. Disclosure and Security

We do not provide personal information to third parties without consent, except where permitted or required by law. External service providers may process information as needed to operate the Site. We take reasonable measures to help protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure, or alteration.

7. Questions About Your Information

If you have a question about how your information is handled, please use a contact method published on the Site. A dedicated contact form will be announced once it is published and tested.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be revised to reflect changes in applicable law, Site features, or operations. Material changes will be announced on the Site.

9. Operator

It does not represent that features which have not yet been implemented—such as a contact form or automatic deletion after unsubscribing—are already available. This policy will be updated as implementation and operations change.
