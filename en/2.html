Kaweco Art Sport
Terrazzo

This article is about a fountain pen I’ve had my eye on recently, Kaweco Art Sport Terrazzo.

The Kaweco Art Sport is a relatively recent fountain pen series from Kaweco.

“Terrazzo,” the model I’m especially interested in, is an Italian word for a composite material made by mixing crushed marble into cement, creating a mosaic-like pattern.

Apparently, each pen’s pattern is made by hand, one at a time, so no two patterns are exactly alike.

Its bold pattern makes it a really appealing design to me.

I’ve been especially interested in this pen lately, I hope to get one by the end of September.
