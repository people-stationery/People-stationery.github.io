01 / ABOUT STARDIO

Turning curiosity into media.

Stardio is an activity brand focused on exploring the things we love and sharing their appeal through our own perspective.

The name “Stardio” combines “Star” and “Studio.” We turn interests and discoveries into content and share them with others.

Stardio currently operates two stationery media projects: “People Stationery” (人文房具) and “Studio People.” Each shares the appeal and stories of stationery in its own way.
