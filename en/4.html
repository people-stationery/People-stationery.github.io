Stationery News
October 22

A roundup of new products, limited colors, and release news from official manufacturer announcements.

Hello, this is Studio People.

Today, we’ve rounded up stationery released today as well as notable models coming soon, based on official information from stationery manufacturers. Release dates and prices are listed according to information published by each manufacturer.

01 | Pilot “Acro Christmas Market in Blue World”

Announcement: October 21, 2026

Expected release: November 2026

Suggested retail price: ¥605 (tax included)

Pilot is introducing a limited-edition oil-based ballpoint pen based on the Acro 300. The collection’s theme is “treasures found at a Christmas market and flea market in a blue world.” It features six original designs, including hand-knitted socks and a lamb snow globe. The pen has a 0.5 mm tip and black ink.

What stationery fans may like: It combines an affordable everyday pen with a distinctive world of its own. The series may especially appeal to anyone who enjoys blue-themed designs.

02 | Mitsubishi Pencil “uniball ZENTO” — New Colors for the 3-Color Pen

Announcement: October 8, 2026

Release date: October 22, 2026

New barrel colors have been added to both the Flow and Standard versions of the uniball ZENTO 3-color ballpoint pen. The Flow model comes in Silver (0.38 mm) and Black (0.5 mm), with a reference price of ¥2,530. The Standard model comes in B Lotus Pink (0.38 mm) and B Marine Blue (0.5 mm), with a reference price of ¥825.

The Flow model features a low center of gravity and a knurled metal grip. The Standard model uses a two-tone design, with different colors for the grip and barrel.

What stationery fans may like: The two versions take different approaches: one emphasizes the feel of a metal grip, while the other focuses on color combinations. It should be easy to choose based on whether you prioritize materials and function or visual design.

03 | Platinum Pen “PRO-USE 171” — Limited Colors

Official announcement: September 28, 2026

Release date: October 28, 2026

Price: ¥4,180 (tax included)

The drafting mechanical pencil PRO-USE 171 is getting two limited colors: Silky White and Silky Pink. These 0.5 mm models combine pearl-resin barrels with a special matte coating and pink-gold-colored parts.

Its mechanisms include the “Shnork System,” which lets users adjust the length of the lead sleeve, and the “Thin Switch Mechanism,” which switches the lead cushioning function on or off.

What stationery fans may like: The design pairs soft pastel colors with pink-gold accents while retaining drafting-pencil functionality. The ability to switch the writing feel is another feature worth watching—not just the appearance.

Read Platinum Pen’s official announcement ↗

04 | Mitsubishi Pencil “JETSTREAM” — Limited Colors for the 20th Anniversary in Japan

Announcement: October 14, 2026

Release date: October 27, 2026

To celebrate 20 years since JETSTREAM launched in Japan, four limited barrel colors selected through a user vote are arriving: Khaki Brown, Beige Brown, Sheer White, and Two-Tone Pink. The standard single-color pen costs ¥165, while the new 3-color ballpoint pen costs ¥550. An assortment set containing all eight items is also scheduled for release at ¥2,860.

What stationery fans may like: The colors were selected through a user vote, which makes this release especially interesting. Even with a familiar everyday pen, new barrel colors can be appealing both for daily use and for collecting.

Read Mitsubishi Pencil’s official announcement ↗

05 | Sailor “Naginata Togi Ebonite Fountain Pen — Enryu”

Announcement: October 14, 2026

Release date: October 24, 2026

Price: ¥275,000 (tax included)

The eighth entry in a series inspired by Utagawa Hiroshige’s “Unryu,” this ebonite fountain pen is limited to 450 pieces worldwide. It features an extra-large 21-karat gold Naginata Togi nib. The ebonite material has a black base with white and gray patterns, accented with red.

What stationery fans may like: Each pen’s unique pattern and material character are part of the appeal. The Naginata Togi nib is also notable for allowing line-width variation depending on the angle at which the pen is held. This is a writing instrument with the appeal of a craft object, quite different from an everyday pen.
