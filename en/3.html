New KURUTOGA DIVE
Color Incoming?!

A new color is coming to the KURUTOGA DIVE!

The new color is, surprisingly, black. It was scheduled to launch on September 16, 2026.

The New Color: Black

The entire barrel is finished in black. The KURUTOGA DIVE has come in several colors before, but this one takes a much simpler approach.

The barrel has a matte black finish.

A Matte Finish

Like the finish used on Twilight Orange, this black version also has a matte texture.

Personally, I prefer a glossy finish to a matte one, so I can’t decide based on photos alone.

I’m curious to see how the finish looks in person.

I’ll Decide After Seeing It

I really like black as a color, so depending on the actual finish, it could become a candidate for purchase.

Still, glossy black is more my style. So for now, I’ll make my decision after seeing it in person.

I wonder what it will look like in person… I’m looking forward to seeing it.

Product: KURUTOGA DIVE

New color: Black

Release date: September 16, 2026

Barrel finish: Matte black
